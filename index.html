I am an M.S. student in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, working in the Statistical Inference & Information Theory Laboratory under Prof. Junmo Kim. My research is in 3D vision and geometric perception: 3D reconstruction, visual SLAM, and multi-view geometry, with autonomous driving as the setting I care most about.

Recent work asks how feed-forward 3D reconstruction models hold up on long, real-world sequences — where their geometry drifts, why it drifts, and what can be corrected without paying for another forward pass. Before that, at GIST, I worked on making reconstruction and feature matching run within the latency and memory budgets of mobile and edge devices.
