3D Vision · Geometric Perception

I work on 3D vision and geometric perception — 3D reconstruction, visual SLAM, and multi-view geometry — with autonomous driving as the setting I care most about.

I am an M.S. student in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, in the Statistical Inference & Information Theory Laboratory under Prof. Junmo Kim. Recent work asks how feed-forward 3D reconstruction models hold up on long, real-world sequences: where their geometry drifts, why it drifts, and what can be corrected without paying for another forward pass.

Alongside that, I am the principal investigator of a National Research Foundation of Korea grant on camera–radar 3D object detection, which pushes the same questions about geometry and reliability into the sensor-fusion setting.

Before KAIST, at GIST, I worked on making reconstruction and feature matching fit inside the latency and memory budgets of mobile and edge devices.
